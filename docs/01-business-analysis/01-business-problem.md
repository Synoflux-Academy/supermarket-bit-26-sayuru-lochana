## Discussion Guidelines

- Describe the problem clearly.
- Do not post passwords, credentials, secrets, or other sensitive information.
- Share relevant error messages or screenshots when useful.
- Explain solutions so other students can learn from them.
- Avoid posting complete submissions for other students to copy.
- Keep discussion related to Master Task #3.
- Student-specific progress should remain in the student's private repository.
